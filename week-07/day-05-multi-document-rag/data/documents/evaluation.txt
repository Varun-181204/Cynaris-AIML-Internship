Retrieval evaluation measures how effectively a system finds relevant source passages. Precision at k is the fraction of the top k retrieved results that are relevant. Recall at k is the fraction of all known relevant items that appear among the top k results. End-to-end query latency measures the time taken to produce a response, including retrieval and language-model generation when both are part of the measured operation. A fair comparison should use the same questions, documents, embedding model, language model, and retrieval settings. A small evaluation dataset provides useful evidence but does not establish general performance.
